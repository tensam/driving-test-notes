# 没保险 = 罚款+扣车，不扣分

## Question
Which sanction can be imposed on the owner of an uninsured car?

## Options
- **A) A fixed fine of €300**
- **B) A fine and loss of points on their driving licence** ❌ (my answer)
- **C) A fine and vehicle immobilization** ✅

## Correct Answer
C) A fine and vehicle immobilization

## Why I Got It Wrong
以为没保险会扣分。保险是车主（owner）责任，不是驾驶行为问题。扣分只针对驾驶行为违规。

## Key Rule
- 驾驶行为违规（超速、酒驾等）→ 罚款 + 扣分
- 车辆手续问题（没保险、没年检等）→ 罚款 + 扣车（immobilization），不扣分

## Key Vocabulary
- uninsured = 没买保险的
- vehicle immobilization = 扣车
- loss of points = 扣分
- sanction = 处罚
