# Abnormally Slow Speed on Motorway

## Question
On this automobile route, what is considered an abnormally slow and unjustified speed for a car?

## Options
- **A) Less than 45 km/h.** ✅
- **B) Less than 60 km/h.** ❌ (my answer)
- C) 60 km/h or less.

## Correct Answer
A — Less than 45 km/h.

## Why I Got It Wrong
混淆了"最低限速"和"异常慢速"两个概念。60是最低限速，45才是异常慢速线。

## Key Rule
高速公路速度规则：
- 最高限速：120 km/h
- 最低限速：60 km/h（低于此需要正当理由）
- **异常慢速线：45 km/h**（低于此且无正当理由 = 违法）

## Key Vocabulary
- abnormally slow = anormalmente lento = 异常慢速
- unjustified = injustificado = 无正当理由的
