# Motorway Walking - Breakdown Exception

## Question
May vehicle occupants walk on a motorway?

## Options
- **A) No, except in case of breakdown, and without going on to the road.** ✅
- **B) No, without exception.** ❌ (my answer)
- C) Yes, on the shoulder.

## Correct Answer
A — No, except in case of breakdown, and without going on to the road.

## Why I Got It Wrong
选了"绝对不行"，太绝对了。实际上抛锚是合法例外。看到 "without exception" 这种绝对表述要警惕。

## Key Rule
高速公路行人规则：
- 原则上禁止行人通行
- **唯一例外**：车辆抛锚（breakdown）时可以下车
- 但必须**待在路肩/护栏外**，绝对不能走上行车道
- 下车后应穿反光背心、远离车辆、到护栏后方等待救援

## Key Vocabulary
- occupants = ocupantes = 乘员
- breakdown = avería = 抛锚/故障
- without exception = sin excepción = 没有例外
- shoulder = arcén = 路肩
