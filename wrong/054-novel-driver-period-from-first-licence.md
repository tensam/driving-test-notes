# 新手期从第一本驾照算起

## Question
You were issued a type A2 driving licence one year ago and now you obtain a type B licence, what will be the maximum level of alcohol permitted?

## Options
- **A) A blood alcohol level not exceeding 0.5 g/l**
- **B) In one year's time, a blood alcohol level of 0.3 g/l** ✅
- **C) In two years' time, a blood alcohol level of 0.3 g/l** ❌ (my answer)

## Correct Answer
B) In one year's time, a blood alcohol level of 0.3 g/l

## Why I Got It Wrong
以为新手期从拿B照重新算2年。实际新手期从第一本驾照（A2）算起2年，不因换类别重置。已过1年→还剩1年。和#001 L-plate同一规则。

## Key Rule
新手期（novel driver period）= 从第一本驾照起算2年，换新类别不重置。

## Key Vocabulary
- "In one year's time" = 从现在起再过一年
- novel driver = 新手驾驶员（驾龄<2年）
