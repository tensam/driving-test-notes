# 窄路会车：带拖车的车 vs 公交车

## Question
On a narrow road where priority is unmarked, a car towing a trailer meets a bus; which has right of way if they do not know which entered first?

## Options
- **A) The car and trailer** ✅
- **B) The bus** ❌ (my answer)
- **C) The one which must reverse furthest**

## Correct Answer
A) The car and trailer

## Why I Got It Wrong
知道规则（倒车更难的有优先权），但不知道car+trailer比bus更难倒车。带拖车有铰接点，倒车方向和车头相反，极难控制；bus虽然大但是一体的，倒车相对简单。

## Key Rule
窄路让行优先级（倒车越难越优先）：
1. 带拖车的车 ← 最难倒，最优先
2. 公交车/卡车（大型单体车）
3. 普通小汽车 ← 最好倒，最先让

## Key Vocabulary
- towing a trailer = 拖着拖车
- right of way = 优先通行权
- reverse = 倒车
