# 限速牌+雾天补充牌

## Question
What do these signs mean? (80限速牌 + 下方波浪线补充牌)

## Options
- **A) Do not exceed this speed if the road is flooded** ❌ (my answer)
- **B) Do not exceed this speed in rain**
- **C) Do not exceed this speed in fog** ✅

## Correct Answer
C) Do not exceed this speed in fog

## Why I Got It Wrong
补充牌上的波浪弯曲线看成了水/洪水，实际是雾气图案。

## Key Rule
S-890 补充牌（教材p.87）天气图案区分：
- 波浪弯曲线（飘散状）= Fog / 雾
- 雨滴 = Rain / 雨
- 雪花 = Snow / 雪

## Key Vocabulary
- fog = 雾
- flooded = 被水淹的
- additional panel = 补充牌
