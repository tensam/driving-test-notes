# Roadworks Sign - Two Lanes Diverted to Opposite Road

## Question
What does this sign mean? (施工标志，两条弯曲线偏向另一侧)

## Options
- **A) The route changes from one to two lanes of traffic because of roadworks.** ❌ (my answer)
- B) A lane has been diverted onto the opposite road because of roadworks.
- **C) Two lanes have been diverted onto the opposite road because of roadworks.** ✅

## Correct Answer
C — Two lanes have been diverted onto the opposite road because of roadworks.

## Why I Got It Wrong
没仔细看图。图里画了两条弯曲线都偏向对面 = 两条车道都被分流到对向道路。

## Key Rule
施工改道标志看线条数量：
- 一条线偏移 → 一条车道分流到对面
- **两条线偏移 → 两条车道分流到对面**
- 一条线分叉 → 一变二

## Key Vocabulary
- diverted = desviado = 分流/改道
- opposite road = calzada contraria = 对向道路
