# 陡坡会车：上山车有优先权

## Question
Which vehicle has right of way on a steep slope where two meet on a narrow stretch where they cannot pass?

## Options
- **A) The vehicle travelling uphill** ✅
- **B) The vehicle travelling downhill** ❌ (my answer)
- **C) The vehicle with the greater Maximum Authorised Mass**

## Correct Answer
A) The vehicle travelling uphill

## Why I Got It Wrong
规则理解完全正确（上山车倒车上山难→有优先权），但uphill/downhill选反了。

## Key Rule
陡坡窄路会车：上山车有优先权，下山车要让行（倒车下山比倒车上山容易）。

## Key Vocabulary
- uphill = 上山 ↑（有优先权）
- downhill = 下山 ↓（要让行）
- steep slope = 陡坡
