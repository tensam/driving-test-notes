# 高速故障车必须上路肩并从最近出口离开

## Question
What must be done when a broken down vehicle is driven at 50 km/h on a motorway and disrupts traffic?

## Options
- **A) Drive on the shoulder and leave at the first exit** ✅
- **B) Always stop on the shoulder**
- **C) Drive in the right-hand lane** ❌ (my answer)

## Correct Answer
A) Drive on the shoulder and leave at the first exit

## Why I Got It Wrong
以为只有速度很低才能上路肩。实际上故障车50km/h低于高速最低限速60，属于异常慢速，干扰交通→必须上路肩→从最近出口离开。在高速上即使没干扰交通，异常慢速也必须从第一个出口离开。

## Key Rule
- 高速最低限速 = 60 km/h，低于此 = 异常慢速
- 异常慢速 + 高速 → 路肩 → 第一个出口离开（即使没干扰交通）

## Key Vocabulary
- broken down vehicle = 故障车
- shoulder / hard shoulder = 路肩
- disrupts traffic = 干扰交通
