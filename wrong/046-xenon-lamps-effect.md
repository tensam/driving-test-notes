# 氙气灯（Xenon）的效果

## Question
Xenon lamps...

## Options
- **A) provide worse illumination for the street, but reduce eye fatigue for the driver** ❌ (my answer)
- **B) increase eye fatigue for the driver**
- **C) improve the street lighting and reduce eye fatigue for the driver** ✅

## Correct Answer
C) improve the street lighting and reduce eye fatigue for the driver

## Why I Got It Wrong
分不清 Xenon（氙气灯）和 Halogen（卤素灯）哪个更高级。Xenon 是升级版，各方面都更好：照明更亮更白，同时减少眼疲劳。

## Key Rule
Xenon 灯 = 全面升级：
- 亮度更高，光线更白，改善道路照明
- 接近自然光，减少驾驶员眼疲劳
- 不存在"牺牲照明换舒适"的说法

## Key Vocabulary
- Xenon lamps = 氙气灯（高级，X = eXcellent）
- Halogen lamps = 卤素灯（普通，旧技术）
- eye fatigue = 眼疲劳
- illumination = 照明
